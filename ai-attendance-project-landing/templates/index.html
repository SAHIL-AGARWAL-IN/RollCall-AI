Runs in the browser on any phone or laptop. Nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Create a subject

    Give it a code like CS101. RollCall makes a QR code and a join link for it.

  2. Students join

    They scan the QR code, take one selfie and they're in. A short voice sample is optional.

  3. Take the roll

    Photograph the class from the front, or record students saying “present” in turn.

  4. Check and save

    Every name is listed as present or absent, and anything odd is flagged for you. Fix any mark with a tap, then save. If the camera can't help, mark the register by hand.

Two ways to take the roll

Class photo

RollCall finds each face in the photo and matches it to the students in that subject.

  • Every face is boxed and named on the photo
  • Take one to three photos from the front
  • Good light, faces turned to the camera

Voice roll call

Record the class while students say “present”. Each voice is matched to its owner's sample.

  • One student at a time, with a short pause
  • Flags a voice that answers twice
  • Students add a voice sample once

Catches proxies before you save

Nothing is saved until you've checked it, and RollCall points out what needs a second look.

A face nobody matched
Someone in the photo who isn't enrolled, or wasn't recognised. They get a red box.
Someone from another class
A student RollCall knows, but who isn't enrolled in this subject.
The same face twice
Two faces that look like one student. The closer match counts and the other is flagged.
A voice that answers twice
One student saying “present” for a friend who isn't there.
A weak match
Marked present, but shown in amber so you check it.

Under the hood

Every face becomes 128 numbers. Photos of the same person land close together and strangers land far apart. RollCall uses dlib's face model, which scores 99.38% on the standard LFW face benchmark.

  1. StraightenPhone photos are turned upright from their EXIF tag and resized to 2048 px.
  2. Find facesdlib's detector finds every face, back rows included.
  3. DescribeEach face becomes a 128-number embedding.
  4. MatchThe closest enrolled face wins, if it's within 0.6.
  5. FlagUnknown faces, doubles and weak matches go to the teacher.

Voices work the same way: each “present” becomes 256 numbers from Resemblyzer, and counts as a match when it's at least 0.65 alike. The same voice answering twice is flagged.

What RollCall keeps

Faces and voices are turned into numbers. The pictures and recordings themselves aren't stored.

Class photos
Scanned during the session and never stored.
A student's selfie
Kept as 128 numbers that describe the face, not the photo.
Voice sample
Optional. Kept as 256 numbers that describe the voice, not the recording.
Attendance
Present or absent for each class, with the date and time.
Teacher passwords
Stored as bcrypt hashes, never as the password.

Built with Streamlit, dlib face recognition, Resemblyzer voice embeddings and Supabase.
